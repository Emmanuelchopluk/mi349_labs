#mi349_labs

I used the accessibility checker to evaluate my portfolio website and received an initial accessibility score of 95. After reviewing the results, I focused on improving the website's color contrast to make the content easier to read and more accessible to users.

I entered my website's color scheme into a color contrast checker and adjusted the colors until they met sufficient contrast requirements. I specifically focused on improving the contrast between the background and text colors while maintaining the overall visual style of my website.
